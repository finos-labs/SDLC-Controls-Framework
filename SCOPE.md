# Scope

SDLC Common Controls Catalog is a collection of methods in the form of documentation for **software delivery in regulated industries** with an emphasis on financial services. The methods describe ways to 1) oversee events throughout the software delivery lifecycle, 2) identify and mitigate risks upon those events, and 3) provide evidence to demonstrate that oversight has taken place. The software delivery lifecycle includes phases from requirements gathering to development, build, test, release, and runtime checks.

--- 

Any changes of Scope are not retroactive.
